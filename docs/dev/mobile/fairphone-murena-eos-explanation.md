# Fairphone, Murena, and /e/OS Explanation

## 📘 Purpose & Scope

This document clarifies the **roles of organizations and products** that often get conflated when discussing “a Fairphone with /e/OS”: the **e Foundation**, **/e/OS**, **Murena**, and **Fairphone**. It focuses on meaning and trade-offs (support, price, warranty), not step-by-step installation. For a broader introduction to Fairphone as hardware, see {{ link_with_abstract("fairphone-explanation.md") }}.

## The key difference: who does what

**Murena** is a private company that develops and maintains **/e/OS** (a deGoogled Android fork) and sells it as a complete product—**hardware**, **OS**, and **cloud services** (for example **Murena Workspace**, a Nextcloud-based offering). Between Murena and /e/OS sits the **e Foundation**, a non-profit that hosts and coordinates the **open-source /e/OS** project itself.

In short:

- **/e/OS** — the open-source operating system (project anchored at the e Foundation; Murena is heavily involved in its development and productization).
- **e Foundation** — non-profit umbrella for the community and open-source governance of /e/OS.
- **Murena** — commercial entity that packages, sells, and supports /e/OS on devices and attached services.

## In the Fairphone context

**Fairphone** is the Dutch hardware maker focused on repairability and ethical sourcing. They sell devices with either stock Android (**Fairphone OS**) or **/e/OS** pre-installed, depending on the offer and region.

**Murena** acts as a **distribution and software** partner. Murena and Fairphone are partners, and Murena has sold /e/OS Fairphones for some time. Murena also handles the **US market exclusively** for that path (Fairphone USA Inc. partners with Murena for sales with /e/OS in the US—per public positioning from both companies).

Practical differences that matter at purchase time:

- **Warranty and OS changes:** Fairphone’s warranty terms have been described as allowing you to **change the OS yourself** (subject to their published conditions). **Murena’s terms** are stricter in comparison and **do not** align with that same freedom in the same way—verify the current legal text for your order and region.
- **Price:** Devices with **/e/OS pre-installed from Murena** are typically **more expensive**; the premium goes to the turnkey bundle and Murena’s commercial stack.
- **Camera app:** The **Fairphone camera app** was initially bundled on **Murena-sold** /e/OS units but **not** on **self-installed** /e/OS community builds—details evolved by model and release; check current release notes and forum threads if this affects you.

## TL;DR

| Piece | Role |
|--------|------|
| **/e/OS** | Open-source, deGoogled Android (e Foundation + community; strong Murena involvement). |
| **e Foundation** | Non-profit coordinating the open-source project. |
| **Murena** | Company that sells /e/OS + hardware + services; stricter commercial warranty posture; US channel for Fairphone with /e/OS. |
| **Fairphone** | Hardware; Fairphone OS or partner-preinstalled /e/OS; more DIY-friendly warranty stance for OS changes (per published terms). |

You can buy a Fairphone with /e/OS from **Murena** (turnkey, pricier, commercial terms) or install **/e/OS yourself** on a standard Fairphone (often cheaper, more freedom, less hand-holding and different bundling for some apps).
